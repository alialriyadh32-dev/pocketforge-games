Last Light: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Last Light ("the game"), made by Pocket Forge Games ("we"), collects and how it is used. If you have questions, email playpocketforge@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress (best depths, settings, unlocked colors) is saved only on your device.
  • Ads are optional. They only appear if you choose to watch one to revive. Google AdMob, our ad provider, collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • If you buy Last Light+, the purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your scores, daily-cave results, chosen light color, sound setting and whether you own Last Light+ in the app's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers an optional rewarded ad: after a run ends, you can choose to watch a short ad to revive once. No ads are shown unless you tap that button. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model, operating system, app interactions with the ad, and diagnostic data, in order to deliver, personalize, measure and prevent fraud in ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking). Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchase (Last Light+)

Last Light+ is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We do not receive your name, card or payment details. To confirm and restore your purchase, the game uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt and purchase history, and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Leaderboards

If you choose to use leaderboards, your scores are submitted to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center under your existing profile there. Those services are covered by Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect information that directly identifies you.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking servers.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Data sent to Google AdMob and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit. Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Data held by Google or RevenueCat is kept according to their policies.

Your choices and requests

You can stop all ad data collection by simply not tapping the optional ad, and reset your advertising ID at any time. Because we have no accounts, we hold no personal data about you; if you want to ask about or delete data held by our providers in connection with the game, email playpocketforge@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
